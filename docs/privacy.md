# Privacy Policy
## StudyCanvas for Law Students

**Effective:** September 30, 2026  
**Developer:** Luke Reed  
**Contact:** coolhandlukere@gmail.com

StudyCanvas for Law Students ("StudyCanvas," "we," "us," or "our") is an educational study application for users age 18 and older.

## Information we process
StudyCanvas may process account information, classes, questions, model answers, typed notes, drawings, annotations, question highlighting, flashcards, study progress, practice exam responses, AI grading results, missed-issue history, preferences, generator settings, subscription status, AI usage counts, and files users choose to upload.

Users are responsible for confirming that they are permitted to upload and use any material they provide to StudyCanvas.

## Cloud synchronization
StudyCanvas synchronizes supported account data across signed-in Mac, iPhone, and iPad devices. A local cached copy of supported study data may be kept for offline use.

## Artificial intelligence
StudyCanvas Pro includes optional AI-powered features. AI is not perfect and may produce incorrect, incomplete, misleading, or fabricated material, including bad questions, wrong answers, inaccurate legal rules or case descriptions, unreliable citations, inaccurate grading, missing issues, or misleading explanations.

When a user intentionally invokes an AI feature, StudyCanvas sends the content reasonably needed for that request to the secure StudyCanvas backend and then to the configured AI provider.

Users should verify AI output against their notes, assigned course materials, instructor guidance, and authoritative sources.

## Academic, instructor, school, and copyright policies
Users are responsible for determining whether their use of StudyCanvas complies with instructor rules, course policies, school or university rules, honor codes, exam policies, copyright restrictions, and other applicable requirements.

StudyCanvas does not determine whether a particular use is authorized by an instructor or institution.

## Service providers
StudyCanvas uses service providers including Supabase for authentication, database, storage, synchronization, and server functions; Apple for App Store distribution and billing; RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management; and OpenAI for user-requested AI features.

## Payments and subscriptions
Apple processes App Store billing. StudyCanvas does not store raw payment-card information for App Store purchases.

StudyCanvas and RevenueCat process subscription status and product identifiers as needed to unlock paid features and support purchase restoration. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction or subscription records under their own policies.

## Data location
The primary StudyCanvas Supabase project is hosted in a U.S. East region. Service providers may process information in other locations as necessary to operate their services.

## Sharing
Private notes, drawings, exam responses, personal progress, and AI feedback are not shared publicly by default. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to share material they distribute through StudyCanvas.

## Retention and deletion
Signed-in users can permanently delete their StudyCanvas account from **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.

The in-app deletion flow removes the active Supabase account, user-owned StudyCanvas database records, private uploaded files stored under that user, and the app's local cached class data for that account.

Deleting a StudyCanvas account does not cancel an Apple subscription and does not erase transaction records maintained by Apple or RevenueCat for subscription administration, purchase restoration, accounting, fraud prevention, or other provider obligations.

Infrastructure providers may retain limited logs, backups, security records, or other information for limited periods under their own retention policies or where retention is reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute purposes.

## Age
StudyCanvas accounts are intended for users **18 years of age or older**.

## Advertising and tracking
StudyCanvas does not display advertising and is not designed to use behavioral advertising or cross-app behavioral tracking at launch. Necessary operational, security, subscription, and service data may be processed to provide the app.
