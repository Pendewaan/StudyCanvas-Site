# Support & FAQ

**Support:** coolhandlukere@gmail.com

## What is StudyCanvas?
StudyCanvas for Law Students is a study workspace for practice questions, model answers, annotations, flashcards, exam practice, course sources, and optional AI-powered study tools.

## Who is it for?
StudyCanvas accounts are intended for users age 18 or older.

## Does it sync?
Signed-in StudyCanvas accounts synchronize supported data across Mac, iPhone, and iPad devices.

## Can I study offline?
Supported class and question data is cached locally for offline study and synchronized when cloud access returns.

## What is Pro?
StudyCanvas Pro includes AI-powered question generation, answer grading, Teach Me Why, missed-issue analysis, and AI-assisted flashcard and study-material creation.

Planned U.S. pricing:
- $7.99/month
- $59.99/year
- 3-day introductory trial where offered by Apple and where eligible

## Is AI always correct?
No. AI can generate poor questions, wrong answers, incorrect rules, inaccurate grades, missed issues, misleading explanations, or fabricated information. Verify AI-generated material against course materials and authoritative sources.

## Can I upload professor materials or old exams?
Only if you are permitted to do so. You are responsible for following instructor, school, university, honor-code, exam, copyright, and other applicable policies.

## How do I manage my subscription?
Open **Settings → StudyCanvas Pro → Manage Subscription**. StudyCanvas opens the subscription-management page supplied for the current purchase, with Apple's subscription-management page as the fallback.

## How do I restore a purchase?
Open **Settings → StudyCanvas Pro → Restore Purchases**.

## How do I delete my account?
Open **Settings → Account → Delete Account**, review the warning, and type **DELETE** to confirm.

## Does deleting my account cancel Pro?
No. Account deletion and Apple subscription cancellation are separate actions. Use **Manage Subscription** to change or cancel App Store billing.

## What does account deletion remove?
The deletion flow removes the active StudyCanvas account, user-owned StudyCanvas cloud records, private uploaded files under that user, and that account's local cached class data.

Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase or subscription records under their own policies.

## Does StudyCanvas contain ads?
StudyCanvas does not display advertising at launch.
