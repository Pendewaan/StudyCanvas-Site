# Age Suitability
## StudyCanvas for Law Students

StudyCanvas is intended for users **18 years of age or older**.

StudyCanvas is an educational law-school study workspace. It is not designed for children and is not listed in the Kids category.

## Why StudyCanvas uses an 18+ minimum

Law-school course materials can involve complex or mature real-world subjects, including criminal conduct, violence, drugs, medical issues, sexual conduct, trauma, ethical dilemmas, and other sensitive facts that may appear in judicial opinions, hypotheticals, user-uploaded materials, or user-requested AI study outputs.

StudyCanvas does not add this material for entertainment. It appears only when it is part of the user's legal study content or a requested study feature.

## In-app capabilities

At launch, StudyCanvas:

- does not include advertising;
- does not include social-media feeds;
- does not include person-to-person messaging or chat;
- does not provide unrestricted web browsing;
- does not provide gambling, simulated gambling, contests, or loot boxes;
- does not provide parental-control or age-verification tools.

Users can create, upload, import, and share study materials. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to use or distribute those materials.

## AI-generated study content

StudyCanvas Pro includes optional, user-initiated AI study features. AI output is based on the user's selected study materials or study request and can be inaccurate. Users should verify important content against course materials and authoritative sources.
